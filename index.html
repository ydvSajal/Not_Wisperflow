Everything a dictation app should do, and nothing it shouldn't.

Global hotkey, floating status bar, and three swappable transcription engines. Your audio never has to leave your computer.

Local Whisper engine

Transcribes fully offline on your CPU. Switch to a cloud endpoint or Sarvam for Indian languages when you want to, never required.

One global hotkey

Works from any app. A floating bar shows the waveform without stealing focus.

Auto-paste

Drops text into the focused field and restores your clipboard after.

Optional AI cleanup

Punctuation and filler-word removal through any OpenAI-compatible LLM you point it at.

History & stats

Searchable history, word counts, WPM, and a 30-day streak chart.

Dictionary fixes

Plain or regex replacements for the words Whisper keeps mishearing.

Set up once, dictate for years.

Community guidelines

Short, because rules nobody reads don't help anyone.

01

Search first

Check open and closed issues before filing a new one, someone may have already hit it.

02

One topic per issue

Bug reports and feature requests stay separate so they're easy to track.

03

Be direct, not harsh

Disagree with the code, not the person. No personal attacks, no gatekeeping.

04

Small PRs first

Run typecheck and lint before opening one. Split large changes into reviewable chunks.

05

No pressure on maintainers

This is a side project. Response times vary, that's expected, not a slight.

06

Credit stays with authors

Attribution in commit history and release notes, always.
